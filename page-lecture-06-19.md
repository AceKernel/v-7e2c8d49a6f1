Application: Arbitrage
Arbitrage is the practice of taking advantage of a difference in prices in two or more markets.
In the long run, arbitrage opportunities shouldn’t prevail: investors take advantages of
such opportunities until the prices are adjusted and they disappear.
In other words, arbitrage opportunities can only exist for an extremely brief period of
time: Almost as soon as they are discovered, they are wiped out as a result of the
response of investors.
The absence of arbitrage opportunities is the driving principle of financial theory.
In economics and finance, we say that an arbitrage opportunity exists if there exists an
investment portfolio that pays off non-negative amounts in every scenario that has a strictly
negative cost.
