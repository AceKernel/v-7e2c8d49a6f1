(8) A × B, where A and B are sets
Meaning: A × B is called the Cartesian product of A and B, it is the set of all ordered
pairs (a, b) such that a ∈A and b ∈B.
Examples:
• N × R is the set of ordered pairs (n, r) such that n is a natural number and r is a
real number;
• R × R is the set of ordered pairs (x, y) such that both x and y are real numbers;
• [1, 3] × [3, 4] = {(x, y) ∈R × R : x ∈[1, 3] and y ∈[3, 4]} is the set depicted
