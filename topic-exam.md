Current official exam instructions and approved personal arrangements take precedence.

## Answer presentation

Use lecture notation and official tutorial methods. Define variables and domains, show necessary calculations and theorem hypotheses, and state the conclusion with units or economic interpretation where relevant. Be concise without omitting justification. Distinguish $>$ from $\ge$, interior from boundary, necessary from sufficient conditions, and local from global claims. Check constraints, signs and original formulas before finalising an answer. No supplementary reference guarantees marks.
