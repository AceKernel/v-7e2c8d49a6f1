## Accuracy and authority

Reviewed topic references are supplementary, not official teaching material, an answer key or a marking scheme. They use LaTeX and distinguish theorem hypotheses and course sign conventions. Original PDFs and official corrections remain authoritative.

Full transcripts, single-page text and problem extracts are automatic search aids, **not verified LaTeX transcriptions**. Fractions, exponents, matrices and diagrams can extract incorrectly; the revision scan uses unverified OCR, including handwriting. Check original PDF pages before relying on extracted mathematics. Page labels are PDF-page numbers; printed slide numbers are separately recorded where verified from a footer.

Tutorial 1 and a standalone final/past-exam paper were not supplied. Revision examples are not a final-exam paper. Personal timetables, access arrangements, student submissions and unrelated courses are excluded.
