Detour: Comparison of vectors
Recall that if x ∈Rn is a vector, then the j-th element/coordinate/entry of x is denoted xj,
where j = 1, . . . , n.
For two vectors y, z ∈Rn, where n ≥2, we define
y = z if yi = zi for all i = 1, . . . , n,
y ≥z if yi ≥zi for all i = 1, . . . , n,
y > z if yi ≥zi for all i = 1, . . . , n and yj > zj for at least one j, and
y ≫z if yi > zi for all i = 1, . . . , n.
Recall also that Rn
+ ≡{x ∈Rn : x ≥0}.
